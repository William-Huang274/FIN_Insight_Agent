---
name: source-comparison
description: Compare company disclosures for a specified reporting period and trace each material change to its source.
---

Start with the company, reporting period and decision the reader faces. Use the uploaded filing or an official disclosure. Record the issuer, filing date, fiscal period, currency and units for each value you use.

Compare the current period with a period using the same definitions. When a restatement or acquisition changes the comparison, explain how it affects your conclusion. Link each central observation to its passage or table. Close with the decision the evidence supports and the observation most likely to change it.
